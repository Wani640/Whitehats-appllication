 A cybersecurity news story I read:

Amgen, a large biotech company, told US regulators that attackers stole company data and patient health information from cloud storage run by outside service providers. The company hasn't said how the attackers got in or how many people were affected, and its investigation is still going.

I found this interesting because it involves health data, which connects to my field. It also shows that a company can be affected even when the weak point is a vendor it depends on, not its own systems.
